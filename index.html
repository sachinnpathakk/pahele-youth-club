Our Story

The Pahele Youth Club wasn't born in a boardroom; it was born on the dusty trails and tea-shops of Pathkhola. We saw the beauty of our hills, but we also saw the gaps—the need for better waste management, youth empowerment, and a unified platform for community service.

Today, we stand as a bridge between traditional wisdom and youthful energy, working tirelessly to ensure that Pathkhola doesn't just survive, but thrives.
