Our Story

The Pahele Youth Club wasn't born in a boardroom; it was born on the dusty trails and tea-shops of Pathkhola.

We saw the beauty of our hills, but we also saw the gaps—the need for better waste management and youth empowerment. Today, we stand as a bridge between traditional wisdom and youthful energy.
