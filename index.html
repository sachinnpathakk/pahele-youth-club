Our Roots

Rooted in Service,
Growing in Strength.

The Pahele Youth Club wasn't born in a boardroom; it was born on the dusty trails and tea-shops of Pathkhola. We saw the beauty of our hills, but we also saw the gaps.

Today, we stand as a bridge between traditional wisdom and youthful energy, working tirelessly to ensure that Pathkhola doesn't just survive, but thrives.

President Kamal Gaihre Vice President Sachin Pathak

Our Legacy in Action

Tangible Contributions

We don't just talk about change; we build it. From civic safety to national pride, our footprints are everywhere in Pathkhola.

Civic Infrastructure

Urban Safety & Welcome

Implemented strategic traffic signage, restored community chautari steps, and placed welcoming boards for visitors, ensuring safety and a warm gesture for everyone entering our town.

Tourism & Heritage

Picnic Spot Restoration

Restored and cleaned our famous local picnic spots, adding detailed information boards to educate visitors and preserve the natural beauty.

Youth Mobilization

National Pride & Events

Installed the national flag as a symbol of youth encouragement and organized 17+ community events including cultural competitions to foster leadership and unity.
